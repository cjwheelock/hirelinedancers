Founding Instructor Refund Policy

The founding guarantee applies only to first-year founding memberships. If Hire Line Dancers does not help an eligible instructor get booked for a paid event in the first membership year, the first-year listing fee may be refunded.

  • The instructor must maintain a complete approved profile.
  • Contact information, service area, and availability must remain current.
  • The instructor should respond to buyer inquiries within 48 hours when reasonably possible.
  • A booking means a paid event generated from a Hire Line Dancers inquiry.
  • Refund requests must be submitted within 30 days after the first membership year ends.

Refunds are limited to the first-year listing fee and do not cover external costs, travel, lost revenue, or payment processing fees unless legally required.
