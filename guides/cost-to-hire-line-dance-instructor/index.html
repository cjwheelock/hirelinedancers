Buyer guide

How much does it cost to hire a line dance instructor?

Many private-event line dance instructors start around $350 to $600 for a short local event, with higher pricing for large groups, travel, premium dates, sound equipment, custom choreography, or multi-hour programming.

What affects price

  • Event length and minimum booking hours.
  • Travel distance, parking, load-in, and venue requirements.
  • Group size and whether assistants are needed.
  • Sound system, DJ/band coordination, or custom playlist preparation.
  • Custom choreography for weddings, brands, or team events.

What to ask before booking

Confirm total price, deposit terms, cancellation terms, travel fees, insurance, arrival time, music setup, age groups served, and whether the instructor is comfortable with your expected crowd size.
