Line Dancing in Austin: From the Broken Spoke to Your Dance Floor

Austin blends old-school Texas dance hall culture with a modern event scene. Here's how to hire a line dance instructor for your Austin wedding, offsite, or bachelorette.

By Hire Line Dancers

Austin has danced two-step and line dances at the Broken Spoke since 1964, and the city's modern event scene — tech offsites, Hill Country weddings, and one of the busiest bachelorette markets in America — has picked the tradition right back up. Boot-stomping is having a moment in Austin, and event planners are booking instructors to match.

The Austin events that book line dancing

  • Company offsites and conferences: Austin's tech and startup crowd wants activities that are actually fun. A 45-minute line dance session is team building without the trust falls.
  • Hill Country weddings: from Dripping Springs to Driftwood, ranch venues and guided line dances are a natural pair.
  • Bachelorette parties: private lessons give the group a reason to wear the boots they bought for the trip.

What to expect

Instructors bring the playlist, the teaching plan, and the energy. Guests need nothing — no rhythm, no partner, any shoes. Most sessions run 30 to 90 minutes and scale from a living room to a few hundred people. See typical pricing in our cost guide.
