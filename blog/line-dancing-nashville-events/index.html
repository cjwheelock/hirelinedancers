Line Dancing in Nashville: The Must-Have Entertainment for Weddings and Events

Why Music City is America's line dance capital, and how to bring a honky-tonk dance floor to your Nashville wedding, corporate event, or bachelorette party.

By Hire Line Dancers

Nashville doesn't just tolerate line dancing — it runs on it. Walk down Lower Broadway on any night and you'll hear boot stomps echoing out of the honky-tonks before you hear the bands. It's the reason Nashville is the number one city in America to hire a line dance instructor: your guests already expect to dance here.

Why line dancing fits every Nashville event

Nashville hosts more bachelorette parties than almost any city in the country, a booming destination-wedding market, and a packed convention calendar anchored by the Music City Center. What all of those events share is a crowd of people who came to have a Nashville experience. A line dance instructor delivers exactly that — without anyone needing rhythm, a partner, or cowboy boots.

  • Weddings: a 30-minute guided lesson between dinner and open dancing fills the floor with every generation at once.
  • Corporate events: conference closers and offsites get a true only-in-Nashville moment that beats another cover band.
  • Bachelorette parties: a private lesson is the group activity that actually photographs well and includes everyone.

What it costs and how it works

Most Nashville instructors teach a beginner-first format: a quick warmup, two or three crowd-pleaser dances taught step by step, and a photo-ready finale. Typical private-event pricing starts around a few hundred dollars — see our cost guide for details. Compared to most live entertainment, it's one of the most affordable ways to give a whole room something to do together.
